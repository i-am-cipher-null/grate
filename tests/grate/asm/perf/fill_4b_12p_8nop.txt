 * followed by 8 batches whose only content is a disabled TEX word. Those 8

 * fill_12b_12p.txt (same batch count, one ALU packet in each) is what an ALU
 * packet costs when the batch it sits in is otherwise empty, and the
 * difference against fill_4b_12p.txt is what an empty batch costs.
 */
